# MTG Master — The All-in-One Commander Deck Builder, Analyzer and Play Platform

**MTG Master** is a complete Commander platform that combines deckbuilding, deck analysis, collection management, natural-language Magic card search, combo detection, opening-hand simulation, matchup analysis, price tracking and remote Commander play in one connected workspace.

Build complete, legal 100-card Commander decks for casual tables, upgraded precons, optimized Commander, high-power pods and cEDH—or use dedicated **Pauper Commander (PDH)** tools built around that format's own rules.

## What is MTG Master?

MTG Master is a Commander-focused deckbuilding, analysis, collection and play platform. It connects the full lifecycle of a deck:

1. Find the right commander and cards.
2. Build or import a legal deck.
3. Analyze its structure, power, bracket and table fit.
4. Detect combos and understand how they affect the game.
5. Test opening hands, mana and early turns.
6. Compare the deck against a specific opponent.
7. Tune it around cards you own, your budget and your preferred pod.
8. Share the list or play it online with friends.

MTG Master is designed for practical Commander questions:

- What bracket does my Commander deck belong in?
- Is the mana base reliable enough to cast my commander on time?
- Do I have enough ramp, draw, removal and protection?
- Which combos are already in my deck?
- Why does this deck struggle against a particular opponent?
- Which upgrades can I make with cards I already own?
- Are my opening hands keepable, or am I relying on lucky draws?
- How can I explain this deck clearly during Rule Zero?

Many Commander players already use resources such as **Moxfield** or **Archidekt** for deckbuilding, **EDHREC** for community-driven recommendations, **Scryfall** for Magic card search and data, **Commander Spellbook** for combo discovery, or **ManaBox** for collection workflows. MTG Master can complement those resources while offering a connected path from deck idea to analysis, collection-aware tuning, playtesting, sharing and online play.

The difference is not one isolated feature. It is the connected workflow:

- **System-first:** mana, ramp, draw, interaction, protection, combos and win conditions are evaluated together.
- **Bracket-aware:** recommendations consider the games and tables you intend to join.

- **Player-controlled:** guided features support your decisions; you decide what belongs in your deck and at your table.

## Commander Deck Analysis

The **AI Commander Deck Analyzer** uses structured analysis to evaluate how a deck works as a complete system, not just whether it contains popular cards.

### What the analysis reviews

- Mana base, color sources and mana curve
- Ramp and early acceleration
- Card draw and advantage engines
- Targeted removal, board wipes and interaction
- Protection, recursion and resilience
- Tutors, fast mana and other power signals
- Commander synergy and dependency
- Combo density and win conditions
- Early-, mid- and late-game plans
- Structural weaknesses and pressure points
- General power and consistency
- Fit within the selected Commander bracket

The output is a readable verdict with strengths, liabilities, scores and actionable recommendations. Reports can be translated, revisited through evaluation history and shared with a public link.

Recommendations are context-aware. A useful upgrade for a Bracket 2 precon is not automatically the right upgrade for a Bracket 4 list, and a collection-first build should not be treated like a deck with unlimited card access.

## Commander Brackets, Power Level and Rule Zero

Commander power is more useful when it describes the game a deck creates—not when it is reduced to “probably a 7.”

MTG Master helps evaluate **Commander Brackets 1–5** using signals such as:

- Expected game speed
- Consistency and resilience
- Tutors and fast mana
- Compact or deterministic combos
- Extra turns, locks and other game-warping patterns
- Interaction quality
- Win-condition efficiency
- Commander dependency
- Game Changers
- Intended table experience

The platform provides both a general deck assessment and bracket-aware context. The goal is not to replace Rule Zero; it is to make Rule Zero clearer, faster and more useful.

## Natural-Language Magic Card Database Search

You do not need to know a card's exact name or Oracle wording. Describe what your deck needs in ordinary language:

- “white spells that exile creatures”
- “cheap blue counterspells”
- “green ramp creatures”
- “cards that protect my commander”
- “graveyard hate in white”
- “token doublers for Commander”
- “artifact removal I already own”

Natural search works across MTG Master's **Magic: The Gathering card database** and your own collection. It understands card roles, effects, color identity, card type, legality, set, rarity, price and ownership signals, and it can be combined with exact filters for more precise results.

This makes it easier to discover removal, ramp, draw, protection, sacrifice outlets, finishers, combo pieces and meta-specific answers—even when you do not know what to search for by name.

## Commander Combo Detection

MTG Master identifies known combo lines in saved and public Commander decks using Commander Spellbook data.

For each detected combo, you can review:

- The cards required
- How the line works
- Result categories such as infinite mana, damage, life, tokens or locks
- Whether the combo is central or incidental to the deck
- How combo density affects bracket and table-fit expectations
- The original Commander Spellbook reference

Combo discovery helps prevent accidental pod mismatches, clarifies the deck's real win conditions and reveals synergy packages that may otherwise be easy to miss.

## Collection Manager and Card Scanner

Deckbuilding happens with real cards, real binders and real budgets. MTG Master connects collection management directly to deckbuilding.

### Manage a real paper collection

- Track owned cards and quantities
- Preserve set, collector number, language and foil information
- Organize cards into custom folders
- Import and export collection data with CSV files
- Review import history and reverse an import when needed
- Search the collection with filters or natural language
- Track alternate printings
- Mark cards as “To Sell” and manage sale quantities
- See owned, missing and alternate-printing indicators inside decks
- Calculate the exact cost to complete a deck
- Prioritize collection-owned cards during deckbuilding and upgrades

### Scan physical Magic cards

The Card Scanner speeds up collection entry by recognizing physical cards and helping you add the correct printing. Set filters and manual set-code plus collector-number entry provide control when a scan needs help.

## Opening Hand Simulator and Mana Analysis

A deck can look powerful on paper and still produce unreliable games. MTG Master provides multiple tools for testing the first turns before game night.

### Opening Hand Analysis

- Simulate batches of opening hands
- Compare keep and mulligan outcomes
- Estimate playable-opening and risky-hand rates
- Detect land-light and land-heavy failure patterns
- Measure early color access
- Review early development and commander castability
- Identify opening-hand bottlenecks
- Connect failures to practical mana-base changes
- Generate a benchmark-linked, collection-aware Mana Fix Plan

### Mana Base and Color Pressure

- Compare colored-pip demand with available sources
- Detect stressed colors and surplus fixing
- Review land count, curve and mana-value distribution
- Inspect early-turn requirements
- Understand whether ramp and fixing arrive at the right time

### Goldfish Play Simulator

Use the turn-by-turn play sandbox to draw hands, take mulligans, sequence lands and spells, manage zones, inspect tokens and pressure-test a deck's early plan without an opponent.

## Commander Deck Matchup Analyzer

Normal deck analysis asks whether one deck is sound. The **Commander Deck Matchup Analyzer** asks how two specific decks play into each other.

Compare a saved deck against one of your own decks, a followed deck or a public deck to receive:

- Estimated matchup advantage and confidence
- A plain-language matchup verdict
- Early-, mid- and late-game phase advantages
- Speed, consistency, resilience and interaction alignment
- Commander dependency and mulligan pressure
- Key threats and must-answer cards
- Structural weaknesses and decisive pressure points
- Matchup-specific mulligan and tactical advice
- Category-first improvement recommendations
- Saved matchup report history

The estimated edge is a structural comparison, not a promised win rate. Commander variance, politics, sequencing, pilot decisions and the rest of the pod still matter.

## Remote Play Table for Online Commander

**Remote Table** connects your saved decks to a synchronized, player-controlled Commander game room.

- Create private multiplayer rooms
- Select saved Commander or Pauper Commander decks
- Keep hands and libraries private
- Share battlefields, graveyards, exile and command zones
- Handle opening hands, mulligans and starting-player flow
- Track life totals and commander damage
- Create and manage tokens, counters and attachments
- Switch between full-table and focused board views
- Follow turn flow and a shared event log
- Talk through built-in pod voice

Remote Table is a manual play environment: players remain in control of rules, choices and social interaction while MTG Master synchronizes the game state. The feature is currently in beta and is optimized for desktop and laptop play.

## Product Principles

- **Context over popularity:** cards matter because of their role in a specific deck and pod.
- **Clarity over mystery:** players should understand an analysis or recommendation.
- **Consistency over perfect draws:** reliable development matters more than one ideal goldfish game.
- **Player agency over automation:** tools should support decisions, not replace them.
- **Collection reality matters:** deckbuilding includes cards, budgets, printings and trade-offs.
- **Table experience matters:** power, bracket, archetype, playgroup and Rule Zero context belong together.

## AI Use and Limitations

MTG Master uses AI-assisted deck building, analysis, matchup comparison, natural-language search and recommendations. AI output is guidance—not official rules advice, a tournament ruling or a substitute for testing and player judgment.

Magic is complex, and Commander is especially contextual. Results depend on the current decklist, pilot skill, local meta, threat assessment, sequencing, table politics and playgroup expectations.
